The daily rhythm

A little rule of prayer for ordinary days.

Doxology gathers the pieces of a prayerful day into one place: morning and evening prayer, readings, devotional practice, quiet notes, and the calendar of the Church.

Morning

Begin with prayer.

Open the day with a simple path instead of a blank page.

Midday

Return to Scripture.

Read with the lectionary and keep the day's feast or season in view.

Evening

Close with reflection.

Keep prayer requests and journal notes private on your device.
