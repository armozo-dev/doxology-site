Doxology Way

A quiet daily rhythm of prayer, Scripture, devotion, and reflection, shaped around the hours of the day and the Church's calendar.

For iPhone, Duo and iPad.

The daily rhythm

A little rule of prayer for ordinary days.

Doxology gathers the pieces of a prayerful day into one place: morning and evening prayer, readings, devotional practice, quiet notes, and the calendar of the Church. Choose your tradition once, Orthodox, Catholic, Anglican, Lutheran or Evangelical, and the whole app follows it.

Morning

Begin with prayer.

Open the day with a simple path instead of a blank page.

Midday

Return to Scripture.

Read with the lectionary and keep the day's feast or season in view.

Evening

Close with reflection.

Keep prayer requests and journal notes private on your device.

Inside the app

Each tradition in its own words.

Every prayer, hymn and reading comes from historic, public-domain books of your own tradition, with the source named.

Prayer

The hours of the day.

Matins and Vespers from Hapgood's Service Book, the Roman Breviary, the 1928 Book of Common Prayer and the Lutheran Common Service Book.

Hymns

A hymn for every day.

The Orthodox saints' troparia, newly translated from the Greek, the Breviary's Vespers hymns, and the Lutheran, Episcopal and Methodist hymnals.

Saints

Who the Church remembers today.

About two thousand short lives and histories, with the saint's hymn, prayer or collect beneath them.

Wisdom

A word for the morning.

St. John of Kronstadt, Thomas à Kempis, St. Francis de Sales, Johann Arndt, Jeremy Taylor and Charles Spurgeon, chosen for the season.

Private

Yours alone.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device and works offline. One purchase, no subscription.
