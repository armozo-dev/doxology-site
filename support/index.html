Common Questions

Why do some Bible features need internet access?

Some chapter and search features fetch scripture text from third-party Bible services. Once a passage is loaded, the app may cache it on your device for quicker access later.

Are my journal entries uploaded?

The current version keeps journal content on your device. If you export or share journal content, your device will use the app or service you choose from the share sheet.

How do I manage reminders?

Reminder settings are available inside the app. If notifications are disabled, open your device settings and allow notifications for Doxology.
