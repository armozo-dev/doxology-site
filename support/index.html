Common Questions

Does Doxology Way work without internet?

Yes. The Bible texts, readings, prayers, and history are all included in the app, so everything works offline, including search.

Are my journal entries uploaded?

The current version keeps journal content on your device. If you export or share journal content, your device will use the app or service you choose from the share sheet.

How do I back up or delete my data?

On the Profile screen, choose Back Up Journal (JSON) or Export Prayer Journal to save a copy through the share sheet. Choose Delete All My Data to permanently remove everything the app stores on your device.

How do I manage reminders?

Reminder settings are available inside the app. If notifications are disabled, open your device settings and allow notifications for Doxology Way.
