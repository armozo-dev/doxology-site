Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Doxology is designed as a local-first daily prayer and scripture app. We do not operate advertising, third-party analytics, or user-tracking systems in the app.

Information stored on your device

The app may store your preferences, reminder settings, completed rhythm items, bookmarks, prayer requests, reflections, journal notes, and cached scripture passages on your device. This information is used to make the app work for you and is not sent to Doxology servers.

Network requests

Some scripture features use third-party Bible services to load chapter text or search scripture. These requests may include the Bible reference, search text, and selected translation needed to return the requested passage. The app currently uses bible-api.com for passage and chapter display and bolls.life for Bible search.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Doxology uses your device notification system to schedule prayer, reading, and custom reminders. Notification permission is controlled by your device settings.

Journal export and sharing

If you choose to export or share content from the app, your device may open the native share sheet. What happens next depends on the app or service you choose from that share sheet.

Accounts, purchases, and cloud sync

The current version of Doxology does not require an account, does not use active cloud sync, and does not include an active in-app purchase or subscription flow.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@doxologyapp.com.
